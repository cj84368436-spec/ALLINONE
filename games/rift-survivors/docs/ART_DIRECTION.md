# Visual release 1.2.0

Original AI-assisted painterly 2D assets now replace the prototype pixel shapes. The approved direction is dark emerald woodland, a copper-haired rune knight in gold/silver armor and an emerald cape, warm golden sword effects, and violet corrupted enemies.

## Included
- Four knight poses: idle, two walk frames, and attack. Ranger and mage have independent character art with procedural movement; full directional animation sheets remain future work.
- Five enemy portraits, a corrupted tree boss and a rune stone prop.
- Painted repeating forest floor, depth ordering, readable outlines, cached hit masks and quality-aware effects.
- Gold crescent sweep, impact sparks, white enemy hit flash, visual recoil, dash afterimages, spell trails, evolving skill effects and layered procedural sound.
- Original SVG skill icons and gold/emerald mobile UI.
- Camera shake and particle movement respect reduced effects. The existing 60Hz simulation, adaptive render budget, entity caps, controls, and saves remain supported.
- Asset-loading failures expose a retry button. Play starts only after required assets load.

## Assets and mobile limits
Original PNG sources are in art-source/. Reproducible extraction and WebP preparation are in scripts/prepare-art.mjs, generated by .github/workflows/rift-art.yml.
Production textures in public/art/ total 504,782 bytes (13 files); original source PNGs are not shipped in the Toss bundle. All production graphics and audio are bundled locally; there are no CDN fonts or remote runtime dependencies.
Sprite outlines, hit masks, glows, sword sweep and ground patterns are cached. Visible objects are culled; decorative effects have a separate limit in lower quality modes.

This release applies the art direction to real gameplay. A generated concept board is not a literal promise of identical composition or detail at mobile sprite scale.

## Validation
The release workflow runs logic tests, Chromium/WebKit mobile viewport tests, stress scene measurements, nine complete legal-input playthroughs, and an Apps in Toss .ait build. Browser engine tests are not tests inside the native Toss app on a real iPhone. Console registration, native device checks and Toss release review remain pending.
